Miside Camera modification, 2026-10-26:
During APK assembly, the RTP sequence wrap arithmetic in
androidx.media3.exoplayer.rtsp.RtpPacketReorderingQueue.calculateSequenceNumberShift
uses a sequence space of 65536 instead of 65535. This prevents valid sequence zero
from being discarded after sequence 65535. The upstream Maven artifact is unchanged.
The modification is applied by android/buildSrc using AGP instrumentation;
all other Media3 classes retain their upstream implementation.
